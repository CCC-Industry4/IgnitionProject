# Smart Bulb

---

1. The Smart Bulb

    ![Smart Bulb](/images/smartbulb/smartbulb0.jpg)

    - You can use the sliders to control the RGB values and light intensity of the bulb.
    - The button on the left allows you to remotely toggle the light off/on.
    - The sliders all have property change scripts, meaning as soon as any one of the sliders are moved, it will update the bulb accordingly.
    - The on/off button has an on-click event, so when it is clicked its state will change.
